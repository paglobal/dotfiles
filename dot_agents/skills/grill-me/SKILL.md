---
name: grill-me
description: Grill plans, decisions, and ideas relentlessly. Use when user asks to grill or stress-test their thinking.
---

- Grill relentlessly until shared understanding.
- Map decisions as dependency tree. User decides; agent finds facts.
- Work in rounds. Ask all unblocked questions; defer questions depending on unsettled answers or research.
- Number questions. Recommend answer for each. Word so “yes” accepts recommendation.
- Research discoverable facts yourself, within permissions. Delegate when available; meanwhile ask independent questions.
- Wait for answers. Update tree; repeat.
- Finish when all branches resolved, no hidden assumptions. Get user's confirmation before acting.
- No automatic glossary or ADR writes.

Round format:

❓ **Q1** — **<title>**: <question>

➡️ <recommendation>

---

❓ **Q2** — **<title>**: <question>

➡️ <recommendation>
